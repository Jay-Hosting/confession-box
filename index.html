Velvet Collar GamesThe Confession Box

Slip a secret through the screen. No names, no accounts. The group reacts, and nobody wins or loses.

Before you come in

  • Adults only. This box is for members of the group who are 18 or older.
  • Only confess about yourself. Don't name, describe, or hint at anyone else, in the group or out of it.
  • A confession isn't an invitation. Reading that someone is curious about something is not a yes to doing it with them.
  • What's in the box stays in the box. No screenshots or sharing outside the group.
  • Hosts read everything first. Anything mean, coercive, or identifying doesn't go up.
  • Reactions are for support. There's no dislike button on purpose.

You'll find the code pinned in the Telegram group.

On the wall

    Seen something on the wall that isn't okay? Message a host in Telegram and it comes down, no questions asked.

    Write a confession

    It goes to the hosts first and appears on the wall once they approve it. Nothing about you is saved, only the words and tags.

    Keep it about you. Leave out names, places, and anything that could point to someone else.
